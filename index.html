About the Course

Overview

This graduate course covers the mathematical foundations of stochastic filtering, state estimation, and system identification, with applications in control, signal processing, and robotics.

Key Topics

  • Probability theory & stochastic processes
  • Bayesian estimation
  • Kalman filter & extensions (EKF, UKF)
  • Particle filters
  • System identification
  • Multi-target tracking (PDAF, JPDAF)

Tools & Software

Practical sessions use MATLAB and Simulink. Students are encouraged to complete MATLAB and Simulink Onramps before the first class.

Evaluation

  • Periodic exercises & problem sets
  • Practical MATLAB assignments
  • Final project / seminar
