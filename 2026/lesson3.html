Introduction: Estimation Theory

Estimation is the process of inferring the value of a quantity of interest from indirect, inaccurate and uncertain observations.

Given a dataset $Y^N=\{y_1,\ldots,y_N\}$, such that $$ y_k=h(x)+\varepsilon_k $$ where $x\in\mathbb{R}^n$. We want to obtain the best estimate of $x$ regarding some performance metric

$$ \hat{x}=\arg\min_x L(x) $$

Mimimum Mean Square Error (MMSE)

Non-linear case
Let $$ y_k=h_k(\theta)+\varepsilon_k $$

$$ \hat{\theta}^\text{MMSE}=\arg\min_{\hat{\theta}}\underbrace{\int\|\theta-\hat{\theta}\|_2^2 p(\theta | Y^N)d\theta}_{L^\text{MMSE}(\theta)} $$

Optimization problem:
How to find the minimum of this function?

Mimimum Mean Square Error (MMSE)

$$ \int\|\theta-\hat{\theta}\|_2^2 p(\theta | Y^N)d\theta = \int \Bigl(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta}\Bigr)p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

So, $$ \Rightarrow \int \frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{\theta}}(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \int (-2\theta+2\hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \int \hat{\theta} p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=\int \theta p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

Mimimum Mean Square Error (MMSE)

$$ \int\|\theta-\hat{\theta}\|_2^2 p(\theta | Y^N)d\theta = \int \Bigl(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta}\Bigr)p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

So, $$ \Rightarrow \int \frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{\theta}}(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \int (-2\theta+2\hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \hat{\theta} \int p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=\int \theta p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

Mimimum Mean Square Error (MMSE)

$$ \int\|\theta-\hat{\theta}\|_2^2 p(\theta | Y^N)d\theta = \int \Bigl(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta}\Bigr)p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

So, $$ \Rightarrow \int \frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{\theta}}(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \int (-2\theta+2\hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \hat{\theta} =\int \theta p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

Mimimum Mean Square Error (MMSE)

$$ \int\|\theta-\hat{\theta}\|_2^2 p(\theta | Y^N)d\theta = \int \Bigl(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta}\Bigr)p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

So, $$ \Rightarrow \int \frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{\theta}}(\theta^\trp \theta -2\theta^\trp \hat{\theta}+\hat{\theta}^\trp \hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \int (-2\theta+2\hat{\theta})p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta=0\\ \Rightarrow \hat{\theta} =\mathbb{E}[\theta |Y^N] $$

Mimimum Mean Square Error (MMSE)

Let $$ y_k=h_k(\theta)+\varepsilon_k $$

$$ \hat{\theta}^\text{MMSE}=\arg\min_{\hat{\theta}}\mathbb{E}[\|\theta-\hat{\theta}\|_2^2\bigr| Y^N] $$

The optimal solution is

$$ \hat{\theta}^\text{MMSE}=\mathbb{E}[\theta|Y^N]=\int \theta p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$

Mimimum Mean Square Error (MMSE)

The optimal solution is

$$ \hat{\theta}^\text{MMSE}=\mathbb{E}[\theta|Y^N]=\int \theta \frac{p(Y^N|\theta)p(\theta)}{p(Y^N)}d\theta $$

 

Obs: For linear models and Gaussian noises the MMSE is the same as the MAP!

Summary

  • ML: $$ \hat{\theta}=\theta_0+(\mathcal{H}^\trp \mathcal{R}^{-1}\mathcal{H})^{-1}\mathcal{H}^\trp \mathcal{R}^{-1}z $$

  • MMSE: $$ \hat{\theta}=\mathbb{E}[\theta|Y^N]=\int \theta p(\theta|Y^N)d\theta $$
