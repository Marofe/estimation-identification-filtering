Probabilistic State Space Model

Definition: A probabilistic state space model is defined as $$ \begin{aligned} x_{k+1}&\sim p(x_{k+1}|x_{k})\rightarrow \text{dynamic model}\\ y_{k}&\sim p(y_k|x_k)\rightarrow \text{measurement model} \end{aligned} $$ Hypothesis: $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_{k+1}|x_{0:k},y_{0:k})&=p(x_{k+1}|x_{k}) \text{(Markov Property)}\\ p(y_k|x_{0:k},y_{0:k-1})&=p(y_k|x_k) \text{(Conditional Independence)} \end{aligned} $$

Nomenclature

$$ \hat{x}_{k|T}=\begin{cases} \text{prediction}, k > T, \\ \text{filtering}, k=T,\\ \text{smoothing}, k < T. \end{cases} $$

Bayesian Filtering

The purpose of Bayesian filtering is to obtain the marginal posterior distribution $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k+1}) \end{aligned} $$ recursively for each time step $k\in\mathbb{N}$.

Bayesian Filtering

Theorem
  • Initialization: starts with a prior distribution $p(x_0)$;
  • Prediction step (Chapman-Komogorov Eq.): $$ p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})=\int p(x_{k+1}|x_k,y_{0:k})p(x_k|y_{0:k})dx_k $$
  • Update step (Bayes' rule): $$ p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k+1})=\frac{p(y_{k+1}|x_{k+1})p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})}{p(y_{k+1}|y_{0:k})} $$

The Kalman Filter

The classic Kalman Filter is the closed solution of Bayesian filtering for linear (or affine) systems with Gaussian Noises.

Dynamic State Space Model:

$$ \begin{aligned} x_{k+1}&=A_kx_k+B_ku_k+w_k,\quad w_{k}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,Q_{k})\\ y_{k+1}&=H_{k+1}x_{k+1}+\epsilon_{k+1},\quad \epsilon_{k+1}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,R_{k+1}) \end{aligned} $$

Probabilistic State Space Model:

$$ \begin{aligned} x_{k+1}&\sim \mathcal{N}(A_kx_k+B_ku_k,Q_k)\\ y_{k+1}&\sim \mathcal{N}(H_{k+1}x_{k+1},R_{k+1}) \end{aligned} $$

The Kalman Filter

Theorem For a linear system with Gaussian noises and Gaussian prior distribution: $p(x_0)\sim\mathcal{N}(\hat{x}_{0|0},P_{0|0})$:
  • The Prediction distribution is Gaussian: $$ p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})=\mathcal{N}(\hat{x}_{k+1|k},P_{k+1|k}) $$
  • The posterior distribution is also Gaussian: $$ p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k+1})=\mathcal{N}(\hat{x}_{k+1|k+1},P_{k+1|k+1}) $$

The Kalman Filter

Theorem With $$ \begin{align} \hat{x}_{k+1|k}&=A_k\hat{x}_{k|k}+B_ku_k,\\ P_{k+1|k}&=A_kP_{k|k}A_k^\trp + Q_k, \\ K&=P_{k+1|k}H_{k+1}^\trp (R_{k+1}+H_{k+1}P_{k+1|k}H_{k+1}^\trp)^{-1},\\ \hat{x}_{k+1|k+1}&=\hat{x}_{k+1|k}+K(y_{k+1}-H_{k+1}\hat{x}_{k+1|k}),\\ P_{k+1|k+1}&=(I-KH_{k+1})P_{k+1|k}. \end{align} $$

Proof: Kalman Filter

Lemma 1 Given the joint distribution $$ \begin{equation} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x} \\ \mathbf{y} \end{bmatrix} \sim \mathcal{N} \left( \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{a} \\ \mathbf{b} \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{A} & \mathbf{C} \\ \mathbf{C}^{\top} & \mathbf{B} \end{bmatrix} \right), \end{equation} $$ then $$ \begin{equation} \begin{aligned} \mathbf{x} &\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{A}), \quad \mathbf{y} \sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{b}, \mathbf{B}), \\ \mathbf{x} \mid \mathbf{y} &\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{C} \, \mathbf{B}^{-1} (\mathbf{y} - \mathbf{b}), \, \mathbf{A} - \mathbf{C} \, \mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{C}^{\top}), \\ \mathbf{y} \mid \mathbf{x} &\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{C}^{\top} \mathbf{A}^{-1} (\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{a}), \, \mathbf{B} - \mathbf{C}^{\top} \mathbf{A}^{-1}\mathbf{C}). \end{aligned} \end{equation} $$

Proof: Kalman Filter

Lemma 2 Given the joint distribution $$ \begin{equation} \begin{aligned} p(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}) =\underbrace{\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{H} \, \mathbf{x} + \mathbf{u}, \mathbf{R})}_{p(\mathbf{y}|\mathbf{x})}\underbrace{\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{m}, \mathbf{P})}_{p(\mathbf{x})}, \end{aligned} \end{equation} $$ then $$ \begin{equation} \begin{aligned} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x} \\ \mathbf{y} \end{bmatrix} &\sim \mathcal{N} \left( \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{m} \\ \mathbf{H} \, \mathbf{m} + \mathbf{u} \end{bmatrix}, \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{P} & \mathbf{P} \, \mathbf{H}^{\top} \\ \mathbf{H} \, \mathbf{P} & \mathbf{H} \, \mathbf{P} \, \mathbf{H}^{\top} + \mathbf{R} \end{bmatrix} \right), \end{aligned} \end{equation} $$

Proof: Kalman Filter

Prediction Step

(Markov Property)

The Bayesian Smoothing

The purpose of Bayesian Smoothing is to obtain the marginal posterior distribution $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_{k}|y_{0:T}) \end{aligned} $$ with $T>k$ recursively for each time step $k\in\{0,\ldots,T\}$.

The Bayesian Smoothing

Theorem The posterior distribution for $T>k$ is given by $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_{k}|y_{0:T})=p(x_k|y_{0:k})\int \Bigl[\frac{p(x_{k+1}|x_k)p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})}{p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})}\Bigr]dx_{k+1} \end{aligned} $$ where $$ p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})=\int p(x_{k+1}|x_k)p(x_k|y_{0:k})dx_k $$ for $k=T-1, T-2,\ldots, 1$ (backward in time)

Proof: Bayesian Smoothing

Note that $$ p(x_k|y_{0:T})=\int p(x_k,x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})dx_{k+1} $$

Proof: Bayesian Smoothing

Note that $$ p(x_k|y_{0:T})=\int p(x_{k}|x_{k+1},y_{0:T})p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})dx_{k+1} $$

Proof: Bayesian Smoothing

Note that $$ p(x_k|y_{0:T})=\int p(x_{k}|x_{k+1},y_{0:k})p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})dx_{k+1}\\ $$

Proof: Bayesian Smoothing

Note that $$ p(x_k|y_{0:T})=\int p(x_{k}|x_{k+1},y_{0:k})p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})dx_{k+1}\\ =\int \frac{p(x_{k+1}|x_{k},y_{0:k})p(x_k|y_{0:k})}{p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})}p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})dx_{k+1}\\ $$

Proof: Bayesian Smoothing

Note that $$ p(x_k|y_{0:T})=\int p(x_{k}|x_{k+1},y_{0:k})p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})dx_{k+1}\\ =\int \frac{p(x_{k+1}|x_{k},y_{0:k})p(x_k|y_{0:k})}{p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})}p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})dx_{k+1}\\ =p(x_k|y_{0:k})\int \frac{p(x_{k+1}|x_{k})p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})}{p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})}dx_{k+1} \quad \square $$

The Bayesian Smoothing

Forward-backward Smoother $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_{k}|y_{0:T})=\underbrace{p(x_k|y_{0:k})}_{\text{filtering}}\underbrace{\int \Bigl[\frac{p(x_{k+1}|x_k)p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:T})}{p(x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})}\Bigr]dx_{k+1}}_{\text{smoothing correction}} \end{aligned} $$

Rauch-Tung-Striebel (RTS) Smoother

The RTS Smoother is the closed solution of Bayesian Smoothing for linear (or affine) systems with Gaussian Noises.

Dynamic State Space Model:

$$ \begin{aligned} x_{k+1}&=A_kx_k+B_ku_k+w_k,\quad w_{k}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,Q_{k})\\ y_{k+1}&=H_{k+1}x_{k+1}+\epsilon_{k+1},\quad \epsilon_{k+1}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,R_{k+1}) \end{aligned} $$

Probabilistic State Space Model:

$$ \begin{aligned} x_{k+1}&\sim \mathcal{N}(A_kx_k+B_ku_k,Q_k)\\ y_{k+1}&\sim \mathcal{N}(H_{k+1}x_{k+1},R_{k+1}) \end{aligned} $$

The RTS Smoother

Theorem For a linear system with Gaussian noises and Gaussian initial prior distribution $p(x_0)\sim\mathcal{N}(\hat{x}_{0|0},P_{0|0})$, the posterior smoothed distribution is also Gaussian: $$ p(x_{k}|y_{0:T})=\mathcal{N}(\hat{x}^s_{k},P_{k}^s) $$ for $k=T-1,T-2,\ldots, 1$ (backward in time).
Obs: given that $\{\hat{x}_{k|k},P_{k|k}\}_{k=0}^T$ is already computed using the Kalman Filter!

The RTS Smoother

Theorem With $$ \begin{align} \hat{x}^-_{k+1}&=A_k\hat{x}_{k|k}+B_ku_k,\\ P_{k+1}^-&=A_kP_{k|k}A_k^\trp + Q_k, \\ G_k&=P_{k|k}A_{k}^\trp (P_{k+1}^-)^{-1},\\ \hat{x}_{k}^s&=\hat{x}_{k|k}+G_k(\hat{x}_{k+1}^s-\hat{x}_{k+1}^-),\\ P_{k}^s&=P_{k|k}+G_k[P_{k+1}^s-P_{k+1}^-]G_k^\trp. \end{align} $$

Proof: RTS Smoother

Note that $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_k,x_{k+1}|y_{0:k})=p(x_{k+1}|x_{k},y_{0:k})p(x_{k}|y_{0:k}) \end{aligned} $$

Proof: RTS Smoother

From the Kalman Filter, we known that $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_{k+1},x_k|y_{0:k})&=\mathcal{N}(A_kx_k+B_ku_k,Q_k)\mathcal{N}(\hat{x}_{k|k},P_{k|k})\\ &=\mathcal{N}\Bigl(\begin{bmatrix}\hat{x}_{k|k}\\ A_k\hat{x}_{k|k}+B_ku_k\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix} P_{k|k} & P_{k|k}A_k^\trp \\ A_k P_{k|k} & A_kP_{k|k}A_k^\trp+ Q_k \end{bmatrix}\Bigr) \end{aligned} $$

Proof: RTS Smoother

From the Kalman Filter, we known that $$ \begin{aligned} p(x_{k+1},x_k|y_{0:k})&=\mathcal{N}(A_kx_k+B_ku_k,Q_k)\mathcal{N}(\hat{x}_{k|k},P_{k|k})\\ &=\mathcal{N}\Bigl(\begin{bmatrix}\hat{x}_{k|k}\\ A_k\hat{x}_{k|k}+B_ku_k\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix} P_{k|k} & P_{k|k}A_k^\trp \\ A_k P_{k|k} & A_kP_{k|k}A_k^\trp+ Q_k \end{bmatrix}\Bigr) \end{aligned} $$ Thus, from Lemma 1, we have $$ p(x_k|x_{k+1},y_{0:k})=\mathcal{N}(\tilde{x}_k,\tilde{P}_k) $$
