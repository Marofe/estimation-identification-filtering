General Linear Model

 

Definition: (Polynomials) $$ \begin{aligned} A(q) &= 1 - a_1 q^{-1} - \ldots - a_{n_y} q^{-n_y},\\ B(q) &= b_1 q^{-1} + \ldots + b_{n_u} q^{-n_u},\\ F(q) &= 1 + f_1 q^{-1} + \ldots + f_{n_f} q^{-n_f},\\ C(q) &= 1 + c_1 q^{-1} + \ldots + c_{n_\nu} q^{-n_\nu},\\ D(q) &= 1 + d_1 q^{-1} + \ldots + d_{n_d} q^{-n_d}. \end{aligned} $$

Neural Networks

 

Model: $$ \hat{y}[k]=g(\varphi[k];\theta)=w_0+\sum_{j=1}^{N_h} w_j\,\sigma\!\left(v_j^\top \varphi[k]+b_j\right) $$ $$ \varphi[k]=[\,y[k-1],\ldots,y[k-n_a],\;u[k-1],\ldots,u[k-n_b]\,]^\top $$

A neural network approximates the nonlinear map from the regressor $\varphi[k]$ to the output $\hat{y}[k]$.

LASSO Estimation

 

Definition (LASSO): $$ \hat{\theta}^{\text{LASSO}}=\arg\min_{\theta}\;\|Y-\mathcal{H}\theta\|_2^2+\lambda\,\|\theta\|_1 $$ $$ \|\theta\|_1=\sum_{i=1}^{p}|\theta_i|,\qquad \lambda\ge 0 $$

The $\ell_1$ penalty drives some parameters exactly to zero $\Rightarrow$ a sparse model.

LASSO: Sparsity & Model Selection

Ridge (L2): $$ \hat{\theta}^{\text{ridge}}=\arg\min_{\theta}\;\|Y-\mathcal{H}\theta\|_2^2+\lambda\,\|\theta\|_2^2 $$
LASSO (L1): $$ \hat{\theta}^{\text{LASSO}}=\arg\min_{\theta}\;\|Y-\mathcal{H}\theta\|_2^2+\lambda\,\|\theta\|_1 $$
  • $L_2$ (ridge) shrinks all coefficients, but keeps them nonzero;
  • $L_1$ (LASSO) sets irrelevant coefficients exactly to zero;
  • zeroed coefficients remove their regressors $\Rightarrow$ automatic model selection;
  • $\lambda$ tunes the sparsity: larger $\lambda \Rightarrow$ fewer active terms.
